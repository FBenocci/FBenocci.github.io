About

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Pisa, associated with the Institute of Information Science and Technologies (ISTI) of the Italian National Research Council (CNR). I study retrieval for agentic search: when the queries hitting a retrieval system come from LLM agents, which issue many correlated sub-queries over multiple steps, an index built for one-shot human queries is no longer the right design.

Before the PhD I was a Research Fellow at ISTI-CNR, in the HORIZON HPC laboratory, where I worked on approximate nearest neighbor search and the compression of graph-based indexes.
